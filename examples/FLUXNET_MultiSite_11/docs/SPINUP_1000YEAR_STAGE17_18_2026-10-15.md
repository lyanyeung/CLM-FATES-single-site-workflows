# Stage 17–18: Eleven-site CIME/Slurm spin-up submission (2026-10-15)

> **Executed workflow, not convergence certification.** Stage17 prepared one-point CIME cases; Stage18 upgraded prepared cases to 1000 model years and submitted their initial 5-year jobs with automatic CIME resubmission. At the last provided `squeue` observation, many non-CH-Aws sites were `RUNNING`, not confirmed to have completed 1000 years. CH-Aws and IT-Noe were separately observed to have failed runs/dependency errors; never count them as successful. CH-Aws troubleshooting is documented [here](CH_Aws_HUMIDITY_FAILURES_2026-10-15.md).

## Source scripts and preservation

- Stage17 authoritative script: `17_11site_spinup500_srun_pmix.sh`. The recorded distributed package was `FLUXNET_MultiSite_11_spinup500_srun_pmix_STAGE17.zip` under `FLUXNET11_spinup_srun_pmix/`.
- Stage18 authoritative script: `18_upgrade_prepared_11site_to_spinup1000_submit_all.sh`, distributed in `FLUXNET_MultiSite_11_spinup1000_srun_pmix_HOTFIX.zip` under `FLUXNET11_spinup1000_pmix_final/`. **Use the HOTFIX, not the original release**. Its original Stage18 version accidentally overwrote the bash loop variable `v` by iterating `for v in ATM LND CPL`, leading to Bash interpreting `CPL` as an arithmetic variable in resubmission checks; the corrected version uses `for comp in ATM LND CPL`.
- Archival goal: commit *the exact executed versions* of both scripts under `scripts/stage17/` and `scripts/stage18/` after comparing bytes with the files saved on IRIDIS6. **Do not recreate an allegedly executed script by paraphrasing commands.** The public Markdown describes the recorded workflow; actual script files and their commit status should be checked independently.
- The scripts target IRIDIS6, username/site-specific paths, and existing case directories. They are not general-purpose portable installations. **Never re-run Stage18 `--submit-all` on an already started simulation:** it has historical state/manifest guards, and no retroactive repair should bypass them.

## Why two stages

Stage17 clones the compiled and working `GF-Guy_FATES_SURF2000_CO2405_1PT` CIME case with `create_clone --keepexe`, then configures individual site surfaces, site DATM streams and spin-up metadata. It uses 5-year segments, restart output and a site-specific constant CO₂ setting. Original Stage17 prepared `RESUBMIT=99` (500 model years). Stage18 modifies *only the existing prepared* `<site>_FATES_SPINUP500_11P` cases to `RESUBMIT=199` (1000 years), then queues their first Slurm jobs.

The suffix `SPINUP500_11P` is intentionally **historical and must not be treated as the current run length**. The authoritative controls are XML/CIME `STOP_N` and `RESUBMIT`: `(1+199)*5=1000` model years if every 5-year segment finishes and resubmits.

## CIME settings and Slurm controls

```text
STOP_OPTION=nyears
STOP_N=5
REST_OPTION=nyears
REST_N=5
RESUBMIT=199                 # initial submission setting only
CONTINUE_RUN=FALSE            # first/startup run; switches on continuations
CALENDAR=NO_LEAP

DOUT_S=TRUE                   # short-term archive
MPI_RUN_COMMAND=srun --mpi=pmix --ntasks=1 --cpus-per-task=1 --cpu-bind=cores
#SBATCH --nodes=1
#SBATCH --ntasks-per-node=1
#SBATCH --cpus-per-task=1
#SBATCH --mem=8G
```

- Model executable: `/scratch/ly3n24/CTSM_FATES_OUTPUTS/GF-Guy_FATES_SURF2000_CO2405_1PT/bld/cesm.exe`.
- Cases: `/iridisfs/scratch/ly3n24/CLM_FATES/cases/<site>_FATES_SPINUP500_11P`.

## Submitted-job observations and known failures

After the 2026-10-15 Stage18 submission, multiple regular sites repeatedly appeared in Slurm `RUNNING` with subsequent `st_archive` jobs `PENDING (Dependency)`. Observed `RUNNING` names included BE-Bra, FR-Fon, GL-ZaH, AT-Neu, FI-Sod, CH-Cha, CH-Fru, CH-Lae, CZ-Stn. Subsequent complete 1000-year results and convergence statistics were **not** supplied. In a different queue snapshot, `st_archive.IT-Noe_FATES_SPINUP500_11P` was `DependencyNeverSatisfied` (job 4286764); IT-Noe therefore **must not** be labelled a completed successful station without inspecting `CaseStatus` and `lnd.log`. CH-Aws failed under three versions, documented separately.

Slurm states: `PD (Priority)` is an ordinary scheduling wait; `R` is running (not finished); `CG` is completing/cleaning; `Dependency` archive jobs wait for upstream success; `DependencyNeverSatisfied` on `st_archive` indicates an upstream dependency cannot be satisfied, usually after an unsuccessful run. A `None` pending reason is not itself a failure. Verify via `sacct` and case logs rather than relying only on `squeue`.

## Read-only status verification (safe on already submitted cases)

```bash
conda activate clm_fates_env
squeue -u ly3n24 -o '%.12i %.48j %.12T %.8C %R'
sacct -u ly3n24 --starttime 2026-10-15 --format=JobID,JobName%45,State,ExitCode,Elapsed
for site in BE-Bra FR-Fon GL-ZaH IT-Noe AT-Neu FI-Sod CH-Cha CH-Fru CH-Lae CZ-Stn; do
  c="/iridisfs/scratch/ly3n24/CLM_FATES/cases/${site}_FATES_SPINUP500_11P"
  [[ -x "$c/xmlquery" ]] || { echo "MISSING $site"; continue; }
  echo "=== $site ==="
  (cd "$c" && ./xmlquery STOP_N,REST_N,RESUBMIT,CONTINUE_RUN)
  tail -7 "$c/CaseStatus"
done
```

When a case has started resubmitting, its current `RESUBMIT` may be **less than 199** and `CONTINUE_RUN=TRUE`; do not reset it to 199 or `FALSE` on active cases. To assess scientific equilibrium, separately inspect archived annual hist files, FATES carbon pools (`TOTECOSYSC`, `VEGC`, `NPLANT_PF` when available), last-cycle drift, and compare final forcing cycle GPP/NEE to site EC observations. Never equate a successful `sacct COMPLETED` segment with 1000-year convergence.
